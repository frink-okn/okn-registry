GlyGen KG is the RDF knowledge graph built from [GlyGen](https://www.glygen.org/), a data
integration and dissemination project for carbohydrate and glycoconjugate related data. GlyGen
is developed by the George Washington University together with the Complex Carbohydrate Research
Center (CCRC) at the University of Georgia, and it brings glycoscience data together with protein, gene and disease data.
